Important metric definitions

GMV: Total value of orders before deductions. It is not platform profit.

Subscription MRR: Active paid seller subscriptions multiplied by the monthly plan price.

Delivered rate: Delivered orders divided by all eligible orders in the selected period.

Average order value: GMV divided by the number of orders. Exclude cancelled orders in the live calculation.
